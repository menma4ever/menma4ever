## About me

I build Uzbek-language model pipelines and the systems that make them useful: data preparation, tokenizer adaptation, continued pretraining, evaluation, local inference, and tool-using agents. I founded UFL and work on ML/LLM projects alongside agent orchestration and model-efficiency research. My earlier work includes Python backend development, applied computer vision, and technical teaching at Digital City Andijan.

- Studying Artificial Intelligence at **INHA University in Tashkent**.
- Interested in Uzbek NLP, local deployment, multimodal workflows, and reliable agent behavior.
- Comfortable working from the **CLI**, in **Docker**, with **Kubernetes clusters and pods**, on **RunPod GPU pods**, and in **Jupyter** notebooks.
